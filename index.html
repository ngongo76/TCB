AI Product Case Studies — Business Data Analyst (AI Products)

Prepared for the Business Data Analyst (AI Products) role at Techcombank — by Kevin Ngo

The three case studies below follow the full lifecycle of an AI product as described in the job description: analyzing the business problem, framing a hypothesis, defining data requirements, building a prototype/MVP, evaluating results, and setting up a KPI framework and handover plan for stakeholders (AI Engineering, Risk/Compliance, Business).

Each project draws a clear boundary: the Business Data Analyst owns business logic, thresholds, and orchestration requirements — while AI Engineering owns architecture and scalability, exactly as described in the job description.

Risk & Governance

1. Credit Default & Fraud Risk Scoring

A credit risk scoring model with AUC/precision/recall evaluation, and a KPI framework for monitoring the model after deployment.
Marketing & CRM

2. Customer Segmentation & Cross-sell

Customer segmentation via clustering, business interpretation of the AI output, and a cross-sell strategy tailored to each segment.
GenAI Prototyping

3. GenAI Customer Service Assistant

An interactive AI customer-service assistant prototype built with vibe coding, demonstrating the conversation flow and escalation logic.
